# DoC bureau strategic priorities — source data

Per-bureau strategic priorities used to populate `focusAreas` on each bureau's
`UnitOption` in `lib/tenant/doc.ts` (see issue #14). Each item below is
`label` — `description`, followed by a source pointer. `label` is the short
chip text shown in the UI; `description` is the longer tooltip/detail text.
Descriptions summarize each bureau's own published strategic plan/priorities
in plain language; verify wording against the current published plan before
using this in an authorized/production context.

## os — Office of the Secretary
- Steward the Department-wide plan — Sets and stewards Commerce's department-wide strategic plan across all bureaus. (commerce.gov — DOC Strategic Plan)
- Enterprise management & modern capabilities — Modernizes enterprise IT, data, and management capabilities Department-wide. (commerce.gov)
- Coordinate bureaus toward shared mission — Aligns the Department's operating units toward Commerce's shared economic mission. (commerce.gov)
- Allocate resources to strategic goals — Directs budget and staffing toward the Department's highest-priority strategic goals. (commerce.gov)

## bea — Bureau of Economic Analysis
- Timely, relevant, accurate accounts — Produces the nation's GDP and other core economic accounts accurately and on schedule. (bea.gov/about/strategic-plan)
- Measure the American AI economy — Develops new statistics to measure the size and growth of the U.S. AI economy. (bea.gov)
- Accelerate statistics releases — Shortens the lag between data collection and public release of economic statistics. (bea.gov)
- Innovate new statistics & methods — Researches new methodologies and data sources to improve economic measurement. (bea.gov)
- Modernize data access & infrastructure — Modernizes IT infrastructure and self-service access to BEA data. (bea.gov)
- Nonpartisan, high-integrity statistics — Maintains BEA's reputation for objective, nonpartisan statistical integrity. (bea.gov)

## bis — Bureau of Industry and Security
- Protect U.S. national security — Administers export controls that protect U.S. national security interests. (bis.gov)
- Preserve strategic tech leadership — Safeguards U.S. leadership in critical and emerging technologies. (bis.gov)
- Balance security with competitiveness — Balances export controls with the competitiveness of U.S. industry. (bis.gov)
- Partner with the private sector — Partners with industry to keep controls practical and effective. (bis.gov)
- Adapt controls to a changing world — Updates export control policy for a changing geopolitical and technology landscape. (bis.gov)
- Advance international cooperation — Works with allies to harmonize export control regimes internationally. (bis.gov)
