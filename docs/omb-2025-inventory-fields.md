# OMB 2025 AI Use Case Inventory — authoritative field list

**Source of truth:** OMB's own published data dictionary for the consolidated 2025 inventory —
https://github.com/ombegov/2025-Federal-Agency-AI-Use-Case-Inventory (`Validation/data_dictionary.md`),
cross-checked against `context/Guidance-on-2025-Agency-Artificial-Intelligence-Reporting-.pdf` (§5) and the OMB cheat sheet.

This is the plain-text source for the field-config cascade (ClickUp 868kam0x3). **Read this file — do not try to parse the PDF/DOCX.**

Everything below is OMB's exact wording: field name, data type, **valid selections**, and **when the field is required** (which drives `showWhen`).

> **Supersedes the earlier version of this file.** The first pass was extracted from §5 of the guidance PDF, which lists only field names and abstract data types. It (a) omitted the answer option lists entirely, (b) collapsed vendor-name and ATO-system-name into their parent rows, (c) modeled "Is high-impact?" as a Yes/No when it is **three-way**, and (d) missed the conditional `Justification` field. The data dictionary below is the implementable schema — **34 reported fields** (25 base + 9 high-impact-only). `agency` / `agency_name` are generated by OMB and are not agency-entered.

## High-impact risk-management fields (9)

**All 9 are required only for use cases that are `is_high_impact` = "High-impact" AND `development_stage` = "Deployed".** Each maps to an M-25-21 §4(b) minimum practice. Note every one of them permits an "In-progress" and a "CAIO waived" answer — these are *not* Yes/No.

| # | Column | OMB label | Type | Valid selections |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 26 | `hi_testing_conducted` | Pre-deployment testing conducted? | multiple choice | a) Yes · b) In-progress · c) Agency CAIO has waived this minimum practice and reported such waiver to OMB |
| 27 | `hi_assessment_completed` | AI impact assessment completed? | multiple choice | a) Yes · b) In-progress · c) CAIO waived |
| 28 | `hi_potential_impacts` | Potential impacts and how they were identified | free text | — |
| 29 | `hi_independent_review` | Independent review conducted? | multiple choice | a) Yes – by another agency office/reviewer not involved in development · b) Yes – by an agency AI oversight board · c) Yes – by the CAIO · d) In-progress · e) CAIO waived |
| 30 | `hi_ongoing_monitoring` | Ongoing monitoring process established? | multiple choice | a) Yes, sufficient monitoring protocols established · b) In-progress · c) CAIO waived |
| 31 | `hi_training_established` | Sufficient, periodic operator training established? | multiple choice | a) Yes, sufficient and periodic training established · b) In-progress · c) CAIO waived |
| 32 | `hi_failsafe_presence` | Appropriate fail-safe that minimizes risk of significant harm? | multiple choice | a) Yes · b) Not applicable · c) In-progress · d) CAIO waived |
| 33 | `hi_appeal_process` | Established appeal process for impacted individuals? | multiple choice | a) Yes, appeal process established · b) Not applicable · c) In-progress · d) Law/operational limits preclude appeal · e) CAIO waived |
| 34 | `hi_public_consultation` | Steps taken to consult end users and the public | **select multiple** | a) Direct usability testing · b) General solicitations of public feedback/comments · c) Public hearings or meetings · d) Other · e) In-progress · f) CAIO waived |

## Conditional-disclosure summary (drives `showWhen`)

- **Stage-gated (pilot/deployed only):** `operational_date`, `contracting_usage`, `have_ato`, `data_description`, `has_pii`, `demographic_features`, `has_custom_code`.
- **High-impact + deployed only:** all 9 `hi_*` fields.
- **Parent-answer-gated:** `HI_justification` (only on "presumed high-impact but determined not"); `vendor_name` (only on vendor/both); `system_name_ato` (only on ATO = Yes).
- **Always shown:** `id`, `use_case_name`, `agency_bureau`, `contact_email`, `is_withheld`, `development_stage`, `is_high_impact`.
- **Pre-deployment onward:** `topic_area`, `classification`, `problem_solved`, `benefits`, `system_outputs`.
- **Optional (never blocking):** `link_to_data`, `pia_url`, `code_url`.

## Mapping notes for the field cascade (868kam0x3)

- **All 34 fields = `level: "omb"`** in `lib/fieldRegistry.ts` — mandatory-when-applicable, non-removable by any bureau.
- **Do NOT set `locked: true` on OMB fields.** The registry is shared across tenants; the mandate is enforced by the cascade checking `tenantHasBureauTier()`. Hard-coding `locked` breaks USPTO/DoW. (This corrects the earlier version of this file, which wrongly said to lock them.)
- The DoC/EO risk fields (`involvesSensitiveData`, `aiDecisionalImpact`, `aiModelSourcing`, `aiHumanReview`) stay `level: "department"` + `locked: true` — a distinct Commerce/executive-order mandate, not part of the OMB 34.
- `has_pii` (OMB) and `involvesSensitiveData` (DoC) overlap in subject but are separate mandates; keep both.
- **Public-reporting handling:** OMB permits removing only the `id` column and substituting a public-inquiry address for `contact_email` in the public CSV — relevant to `lib/ombExport.ts`, not to intake.
- Department (`os`) and bureau tiers layer on top; neither can remove an OMB field.
- **`is_withheld` (field #5, issue #116):** captured directly as `isWithheld` (`lib/steps.ts`/`lib/fieldRegistry.ts`) with all four of OMB's exact values — `no` / `yes_risk_to_disclosure` / `yes_disclosure_prohibited` / `other` — instead of the earlier two-way `publicIndicator` public/excluded flag. Existing submissions migrate at read time (`lib/formDataMigrations.ts`): legacy `"public"` -> `"no"`, legacy `"excluded"` -> `"other"` (the old flag never recorded *why*, so "Other" is the only faithful mapping).

## Context worth knowing

- 2025 inventory totals: 3,611 individually-reported use cases across 41 agencies; 445 high-impact. **Department of Commerce reported 223 use cases, 0 high-impact.**
- Reporting splits into **individually-reported** vs **consolidated COTS** (widely-used commercial AI, reported once department-wide). A high-impact use case is **never** eligible for consolidated reporting. (Consolidation logic already lives in `lib/ombConsolidation.ts`.)
- OMB explicitly reduced the field count for 2025 to lower agency burden.
