Three use cases, one problem

All three score supplier or vendor past performance before award. Decide whether they are one effort with a lead, or three efforts that stay separate. Neither choice touches the submissions themselves.

Plumb recommends

Keep them separate, and link them.

01 The match is carried almost entirely by the solution — all three build a risk score from CPARS history. The problems they solve diverge.
02 Two serve contracting officers before award; the Logistics entry serves supply planners across the full lifecycle. One tool would have to satisfy both approval chains.
03 The shared piece worth reusing is the scoring model, not the use case. Linking them keeps that visible without merging three governance records.
If you consolidate instead, the inventory count drops from three to one and the other two program offices lose their own approval record. That is hard to undo after sign-off.
